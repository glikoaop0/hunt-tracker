Good to know

  • Linked hunts. Add or unlink hunts from the campaign page. Unlinking removes the hunt from this campaign only; the hunt and its runs are kept.
  • Status. Planned, Active or Closed. Closing never retires linked hunts, and a closed campaign can be reopened at any time.
  • Conclusion. Can also be written directly on the campaign's Overview tab, once hunts and runs give you something to conclude.
  • Last activity. Saving here counts as campaign activity, alongside runs logged on linked hunts.
